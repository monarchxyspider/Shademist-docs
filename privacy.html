Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026


1. Introduction

Thank you for using ShadeMist. Protecting your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information ShadeMist may access, how it is used, and how we protect your data while providing moderation and utility features for Discord servers.


2. Information We Collect

ShadeMist may collect and process limited information required for its functionality, including:

• Discord User IDs
• Discord Server IDs
• Discord Channel IDs
• Discord Role IDs
• Bot configuration settings
• Moderation logs (if enabled)


3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used only to provide the services requested by server administrators, including moderation commands, server configuration, logging, permission management and future bot features.


4. Data Storage

Only the minimum amount of data necessary for the operation of ShadeMist is stored. We never intentionally collect personal conversations, passwords, payment information or any confidential personal data.


5. Data Sharing

ShadeMist does not sell, trade, rent or share your information with third parties except where required by law or necessary to provide the service.


6. Data Security

Reasonable security measures are used to protect stored information from unauthorized access, modification or disclosure. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.


7. Data Removal

Server owners may request the removal of stored configuration data by contacting the ShadeMist Support Server.


8. Third-Party Services

ShadeMist operates through Discord and may rely on Discord's API. Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.


9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect improvements, new features or legal requirements. Continued use of ShadeMist indicates acceptance of any updated version.


10. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data removal, please join our official Support Server.
