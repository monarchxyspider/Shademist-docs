Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ShadeMist handles information when you use the ShadeMist Discord bot or visit this website.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features being used, ShadeMist may process limited Discord-related information required for the bot to function.

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord server IDs
  • Channel IDs
  • Role IDs
  • Information provided through bot commands
  • Server configuration settings

2. Why Information Is Used

Information may be used only when necessary to provide, maintain, secure and improve ShadeMist's features.

3. Server Configuration

Some ShadeMist features may require storing server configuration, such as selected channels, roles, moderation settings or welcome settings.

4. Messages and Commands

ShadeMist may process messages or command interactions when required to respond to commands or perform a requested feature.

We do not intend to use message content for advertising or sell it to third parties.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information to third parties. Information may only be shared when reasonably necessary to operate the service, comply with legal requirements, or protect the security of ShadeMist.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by ShadeMist. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant ShadeMist features, maintain configurations, resolve issues, or meet legitimate operational requirements.

8. Data Removal

If you want server-related configuration or stored information associated with your server to be removed, contact the ShadeMist team through the support server.

9. Third-Party Services

ShadeMist operates through Discord and may rely on third-party infrastructure or services to provide its functionality. Their own privacy policies may also apply.

10. Children's Privacy

ShadeMist is not intended to knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children. Users should follow the age requirements and rules established by Discord.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ShadeMist's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this website.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the ShadeMist team through our support server.
