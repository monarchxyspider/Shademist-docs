Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Thank you for using ShadeMist. Protecting your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information ShadeMist may access, how it is used, and how we protect your data while providing moderation and utility features for Discord servers.

2. Information We Collect

ShadeMist may collect and process limited information required for its functionality, including:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Server IDs
  • Discord Channel IDs
  • Discord Role IDs
  • Bot configuration settings
  • Moderation logs, if enabled

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected by ShadeMist is used only to provide and improve the services requested by server administrators, including moderation commands, server configuration, logging, permission management and future bot features.

4. Data Storage

Only the minimum amount of data necessary for the operation of ShadeMist is stored. We do not intentionally collect personal conversations, passwords, payment information or confidential personal data.

5. Data Sharing

ShadeMist does not sell, trade, rent or intentionally share your information with third parties except where required by law or necessary to operate the service.

6. Data Security

Reasonable security measures are used to protect stored information from unauthorized access, modification or disclosure. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Removal

Server owners may request the removal of stored ShadeMist configuration data by contacting the ShadeMist Support Server.

8. Third-Party Services

ShadeMist operates through Discord and may rely on Discord's API. Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect improvements, new features or legal requirements. Continued use of ShadeMist after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data removal, please join our official Support Server.
